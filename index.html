About me

Strategic thinking. Creative execution. Measurable outcomes.

I believe marketing has real power when insight, creativity and responsibility work together.

I enjoy owning the complete marketing journey, from defining the proposition and audience to executing the launch, reporting performance and improving the next phase. For me, the creative idea and the commercial objective should always strengthen one another.

My experience spans high-profile Indian consumer brands, luxury hospitality, influencer marketing and agency accounts. It has taught me how to adapt the strategy to each audience without diluting the brand, and how to turn a plan into coordinated work across paid media, web, content, PR, email, video and design.
