A café stop is sometimes the whole plan

PREVIEW IDEA · This is a format for a future visit, not a review of a real café. The photograph is illustrative.

Some outings need a packed itinerary. Others just need a good table and someone you can talk to for hours.

What we’ll share

  • Where we went and when we visited.
  • What we ordered, including vegetarian choices where available.
  • The actual bill and whether anything was gifted.
  • The atmosphere, service and who would enjoy it.
  • Whether we would return, and why.

More than a pretty plate

A beautiful photograph is a starting point. The useful part is knowing whether the food, price and experience matched the promise.
