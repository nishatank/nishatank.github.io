A slower day in London

ITINERARY PREVIEW · An illustrative route to develop and try, not a report of a completed trip. Check opening times, accessibility, transport and costs before travelling.

A good day out doesn’t have to mean seeing everything. This draft leaves space for a view, a long walk and a little time to simply be somewhere new.

The shape of the day

WhenThe idea
MorningBegin around Westminster, then cross towards the South Bank for a riverside wander.
LunchChoose a café or restaurant along your route. Confirm vegetarian choices, prices and availability before you go.
AfternoonKeep following the river at your own pace, or choose one indoor stop if the weather changes.
Before heading homePause somewhere with a view. Write down one moment you want to remember.

Make it your own

Choose your start and finish around your accommodation or station. Reduce the walking if needed, and leave a generous buffer for the journey home.

Details we’ll add after our visit

  • Our exact route, date and walking distances.
  • What we spent, with transport, food and tickets separated.
  • The places we actually tried and what we would skip.
  • Our own photographs and accessibility observations.
