SERIES PREVIEW · A light-hearted format to personalise with Nisha and Priya’s actual conversations.

The idea

Show the same small decision from both sides. Priya brings the calmer pace; Nisha brings the energy. Neither has to be the sensible one or the difficult one.

Try it with

  • A free Saturday: one café or three neighbourhoods?
  • A photograph: one candid moment or another ten attempts?
  • A trip: a detailed plan or room to decide on the day?

Make the ending kind

Share what each sister brings to the day. Keep the humour affectionate, and publish only what both are comfortable sharing.
