Life between places · Editorial preview

The life you once wished for

EDITORIAL PREVIEW · Proposed copy for Nisha and Priya to personalise before publishing as their own story.

Somewhere between growing up, chasing dreams and figuring life out, it can become easy to forget how much we once wished for the life we’re living today.

The streets we hoped to walk. The places we only knew through pictures. The small freedoms we imagined having one day.

You can be grateful and still be figuring it out

There is room for both. You can miss home and enjoy where you are. You can feel proud of yourself and still wonder what comes next. One feeling doesn’t cancel out the other.

Maybe today doesn’t need a big adventure. Maybe it just needs a moment of attention: a walk without rushing, a familiar voice on the phone, the light falling across your room.

A small thing to try

Take one photograph of something ordinary that you would miss if your life changed. Write a sentence about why it matters. Keep it for yourself, or send it to someone who will understand.

Some of your ordinary days were once your biggest dreams. They deserve to be noticed too.
